About Me

Turning ideas into reliable, well-crafted software

I'm a full stack developer who enjoys the full journey of building a product — from planning the database schema to polishing the last pixel of the interface. I care about writing code that's easy to read, easy to maintain, and built to last.

My development journey started with curiosity about how websites work, and grew into hands-on experience across the MERN stack, relational databases, and REST API design. Along the way I've learned that good software is less about clever tricks and more about clear thinking and steady iteration.

Right now my goal is to keep growing as a full stack engineer — contributing to products that solve real problems, working with teams that value craft, and eventually leading projects from concept to launch.
